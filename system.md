# Welcome to GMC Backbenchers: The Ultimate Student Collaboration Platform

Welcome to **GMC Backbenchers**! This guide is designed to help you understand what this platform is all about and how you can use it to enhance your learning, collaborate with peers, and showcase your skills. 

Whether you're a student trying to get answers, a teacher grading assignments, or an administrator managing an institution, this guide covers everything you need to know in simple, non-technical terms.

---

## What is GMC Backbenchers?

GMC Backbenchers is a collaborative educational platform. It brings students, teachers, and institution administrators together in one digital space. It goes beyond just sharing class notes; it provides a vibrant community where you can ask questions, find mentors, showcase your personal projects, and track your academic progress.

---

## 🎭 User Roles: Who Are You?

When you log into the platform, your experience is tailored to your specific role:

1. **Student** 🎓
   - *Your Goal:* Learn, collaborate, and grow.
   - *What you can do:* View your enrolled subjects, submit assignments, ask and answer questions in the discussion forum, request mentorship from senior students, and build a portfolio of your personal projects in the Talent Showcase.

2. **Teacher** 👨‍🏫
   - *Your Goal:* Guide and evaluate students.
   - *What you can do:* Create assignments, grade student submissions, answer student questions in the forum, and manage the resources and announcements for the subjects you teach.

3. **Institution Admin** 🏢
   - *Your Goal:* Manage your school or college's operations.
   - *What you can do:* Create semesters, add subjects, assign teachers to subjects, and manage student enrollments for your specific institution.

4. **Super Admin** 👑
   - *Your Goal:* Oversee the entire platform.
   - *What you can do:* Manage all institutions, view platform-wide statistics, and manage overall system settings.

---

## 🌟 Core Features: What Can You Do?

Here is a breakdown of the main tools available to you on the platform:

### 1. 💬 The Q&A Forum (Discussions)
Stuck on a problem? The Q&A forum is the place to go!
- **Ask Questions:** You can ask questions specific to a subject or general questions for everyone.
- **Stay Anonymous:** Feeling shy? You can choose to post questions or answers *anonymously*. The system will generate a fun, random name for you (like *CuriousPanda42*).
- **Vote:** You can upvote good questions and helpful answers, or downvote unhelpful ones.
- **Accept Answers:** If someone gives you the perfect solution, you can mark their answer as "Accepted" so others know it's the correct solution.

### 2. 📚 Assignments & Submissions
A streamlined way to handle coursework.
- **For Teachers:** Create assignments, set due dates, and decide if you'll accept late submissions. You can review student work and assign grades (scores).
- **For Students:** Easily see what assignments are due, upload your files or text, and view your grades once the teacher has reviewed them.

### 3. 🤝 Mentorship Board
Need help from someone who's been there? 
- Students can request mentorship sessions from other students (seniors). 
- If you are good at a topic, you can accept mentorship requests, help your peers, and earn **Mentor Badges** for your profile!

### 4. 🎨 Talent Showcase
Your personal portfolio to shine!
- The Talent Showcase allows students to publish their personal projects, side-hustles, or research.
- You can list the technologies you used (e.g., Laravel, React) and provide links to your work. This is a great way to build a resume and show off your skills to peers and teachers.

### 5. 📢 Resources & Announcements
Stay up to date.
- Teachers can post important announcements or upload study materials and resources for their subjects.
- Students will see these updates on their dashboard, ensuring they never miss an important deadline or study guide.

### 6. 📊 Dashboards
When you log in, you are greeted with a customized dashboard:
- **Students** see their enrolled subjects, recent assignments, and upcoming deadlines.
- **Teachers** see statistics on how many questions are being asked in their subjects and how many assignments need grading.
- **Admins** see high-level statistics like the number of active students, teachers, and subjects in their institution.

## 🛡️ Privacy and Safety

- **Anonymous Mode:** We understand that asking questions can sometimes be intimidating. Our anonymous mode protects your real identity while still letting you participate in the community.
- **Data Separation:** If there are multiple schools (institutions) using the platform, data is strictly separated. Students and teachers only see information relevant to their own institution.

---

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. **Log In:** Use your email and password to access the platform.
2. **Check Your Dashboard:** Your dashboard is your home base. It will show you what needs your attention today.
3. **Explore:** Click around! Visit the Q&A forum to see what others are discussing, or check out the Talent Showcase to get inspired by your peers' projects.

Enjoy your time on GMC Backbenchers! If you need technical help, please reach out to your institution administrator.
